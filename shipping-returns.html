Shipping & Returns

Last updated August 13, 2026

Thank you for supporting a small faith-driven brand. Here’s what to expect after you order.

Made to order

Each item is printed for you individually, so orders aren’t shipped from a warehouse shelf. Please allow 2–5 business days for production before your order ships.

Shipping times & cost

  • After production, delivery typically takes 3–7 business days within the U.S.; international times vary.
  • Complimentary shipping on orders over $75. Otherwise, shipping is calculated at checkout.
  • You’ll receive tracking by email as soon as your order is on its way.

Returns & replacements

Because every piece is made to order, we don’t accept returns for buyer’s remorse or an incorrectly chosen size — please use the size guide on each product page before ordering. However, your satisfaction matters:

  • If your item arrives damaged, defective, or misprinted, contact us within 7 days of delivery with a photo and your order number, and we’ll make it right with a free replacement or refund.
  • If a package is lost in transit, reach out and we’ll help track it down or reprint it.
  • Orders returned due to an incorrect address provided at checkout may require reshipping at additional cost.

Start a claim

Email hello@faithlabel.shop or message us on Instagram with your order number and a photo (for damaged items). We’re a small team and reply as quickly as we can.
